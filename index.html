FighterZone Taekwondo Academy

Taekwondo is a Korean martial art built on discipline, speed, and precise technique, blending powerful kicks with a strong focus on self-control and respect. It's as much about building character and confidence as it is about physical skill.

FighterZone Taekwondo Academy brings this tradition to life with ten years of dedicated training, guided by a coach with over a decade of teaching experience. Its students have gone on to win medals at state and national level competitions.

What you learn

  • Kicks and strikes

    Front kicks, roundhouses, and spinning techniques, built on stance and balance.

  • Forms (Poomsae)

    Patterns that string together stances, blocks, and strikes by belt level.

  • Sparring

    Controlled point sparring for timing, distance, and instinct.

  • Discipline

    Courtesy, integrity, perseverance, self-control, indomitable spirit.

About Us

FighterZone Taekwondo Academy was founded in 2016, and for the past ten years it has built a reputation for developing disciplined, skilled martial artists. Before starting the academy, our coach spent years teaching taekwondo in Lohagarh, Bharatpur, bringing nearly a decade of coaching experience to FighterZone since its inception. Under this experienced guidance, students at the academy have gone on to win numerous medals, ranging from state-level competitions to national championships, reflecting the academy's commitment to excellence both on and off the mat.
