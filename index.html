What you learn

  • Kicks and strikes

    Front kicks, roundhouses, and spinning techniques, built on stance and balance.

  • Forms (Poomsae)

    Patterns that string together stances, blocks, and strikes by belt level.

  • Sparring

  • Discipline

    Courtesy, integrity, perseverance, self-control, indomitable spirit.

About Us

FighterZone Taekwondo Academy was founded in 2021, and for the past five years it has built a reputation for developing disciplined, skilled martial artists. Before starting the academy, our coach spent years teaching taekwondo in Lohagarh, Bharatpur, bringing nearly a decade of coaching experience to FighterZone since its inception. Under this experienced guidance, students at the academy have gone on to win numerous medals, ranging from state-level competitions to national championships, reflecting the academy's commitment to excellence both on and off the mat.
